## 6. Definitions and method

For each record take its key relation: for an item, the strongest relation of its key links; for a step, its own relation.

| Class | Relations | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **1-to-1** | `exactMatch` | [L] states or establishes exactly this |
| **Faithful** | `closeMatch`, `broadMatch` | the same up to a named difference, or [L] proves more |
| **Partial** | `hasPart` | [L] carries part of it |
| **Deviates** | `narrowMatch`; a confirmed finding or erratum; `relatedMatch` where [L] argues differently | [L] proves less, or something else, or the text is wrong |
| **Absent** | `noMatch` | searched for, not in [L] |
| **Unjudged** | none | no completed verdict |

**"Formalized" means 1-to-1 + Faithful.** Report every percentage with its count, as
"41/52 (79%)". Never report a percentage without its denominator.

AI agents judged every relation: for the numbered items a mapper and an independent verifier per batch, a judge on disagreement and an independent review with re-judging; for the displays and proof steps a mapper and an independent verifier per batch, a judge on every disagreement of the first batches and then only on those crossing the formalized boundary, and a judge's re-search of one agreed absence in five. Verdicts were checked mechanically (every declaration against an index built from [L]'s source at the pin, every line range and snippet against that source) and by random 5% samples read by the session leads, themselves agents; a claim that the text is wrong counts only when two independent skeptics confirmed it. No person has reviewed any verdict.

How the class is taken here. An item with a checked absence is Absent. An item with a confirmed finding is Deviates whatever its relation, including the no-match findings. The 17 items that [L] carries inside a proof (status `inlined`, key relation `relatedMatch`: [L] argues the same way but states no declaration of them) are Partial. The 9 items whose verdict was contested or left unsettled (status `unclear`) carry no relation and are Unjudged. Every `relatedMatch` step or unnumbered display is Deviates: by `docs/steps-mapping.md` it means [L] does the step there by another argument or with another quantity.
